1. Derive a formula to compute life expectancy of a continent (a group of countries)

Life expectancy \(e_i\) of country \(i\) is the mean length of life of its \(p_i\) inhabitants, so the total number of years lived by its population is \(p_i \, e_i\). The life expectancy of a continent \(C\) is the mean length of life over all people of \(C\), that is, the total years lived divided by the total population:

\[ e_C = \frac{\sum_{i \in C} p_i \, e_i}{\sum_{i \in C} p_i} \]

This is the mean of country life expectancies weighted by population. A simple (unweighted) mean would give the same importance to small and large countries, while we want the mean over people, not over countries.

# Life expectancy for a continent for each year
# Mean of life expectancies weighted by population
continent_life_exp <- function(df) {
  df |>
    group_by(year) |>
    summarise(
      lifeExp = weighted.mean(lifeExp, pop)
    )
}
